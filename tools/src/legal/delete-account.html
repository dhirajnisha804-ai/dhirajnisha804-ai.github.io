Delete your Derma Desk account

Derma Desk · made by Dr. Nisha Pattewar, Mumbai

Fastest way: open the Derma Desk app → More → Delete account → tap Delete my account twice to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately (if it was long since you signed in, the app may ask you to sign in again first).

If you can no longer open the app

Send an email to dhirajnisha804@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my Derma Desk account”. We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name, email, mobile, password hash).
  • Your profile and professional verification details (registration number, council, college/hospital, qualification, clinic address, verification decision and any proof photo still stored).

What is not on our server

Patient records, photos, prescriptions, notes, scores and MR contacts are stored only on your phone, never on our server. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

What we may keep

Only records we are required to keep by Indian law (for example, a log that a deletion request was received), for no longer than the law requires.
