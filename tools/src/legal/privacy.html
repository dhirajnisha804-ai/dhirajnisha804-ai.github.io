Privacy policy

Derma Desk · last updated 8 October 2026

In short: your patients' records, photos and prescriptions stay on your own phone. We only keep your account and verification details on our server, use them only to run the app, never sell them and never show ads.

1. Who we are

Derma Desk is a dermatology reference and prescription app made by Dr. Nisha Pattewar, Mumbai, India ("we"). Contact: dhirajnisha804@gmail.com. This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules under it.

2. What we collect and why

  • Account details — name, email, mobile number, date of birth, sex, password (stored only as a secure hash by Google Firebase). Used to sign you in and contact you about your account.
  • Professional verification — role (resident / dermatologist), medical council, registration number, college or hospital, qualification, clinic address and a photo of your registration proof. Used only to confirm that you are a doctor before opening the doctors-only content. The proof photo is deleted once your verification is decided; the decision and your registration number are kept.
  • Technical data — basic error and usage information needed to keep the service working and secure.

3. What we do not collect

Patient names, mobile numbers, photos, diagnoses, prescriptions, notes, scores and your MR contacts are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our server. Backups you export are files you control. You, as the treating doctor, are the data fiduciary for your patients' information and are responsible for their consent and for keeping your phone and backup files safe.

4. Who we share with

We do not sell or rent personal data and there is no advertising. Data is processed by Google Firebase (authentication, database and storage) on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by Indian law or a lawful order.

5. How long we keep it

Account and verification details are kept while your account is active. When you delete your account (More → Account → Delete account) they are deleted from our server, except records we must keep by law.

6. Your rights

You can view and correct your profile in the app, withdraw consent by deleting your account, and ask us for a summary of your data or raise a grievance by writing to the email above. We aim to reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), access to shared content is limited by role, screenshots are blocked on sensitive screens. No system is completely secure; please keep your phone locked and your backups private.

8. Children

Derma Desk is for registered medical practitioners and residents only and is not meant for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We will show a notice in the app when this policy changes in an important way.
