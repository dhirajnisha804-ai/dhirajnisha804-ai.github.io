## Points for a dermatologist to decide

1. **Scope:** Khopkar Appendix I ('Office aids': glass slide/diascope, measuring tape, filter paper, cotton swab stick, etc.) was **not** included. Add it if wanted.
2. **Seminar/PPT-only items included:** Adson-Brown, DeBakey, towel clip, Mayo scissors, suture-removal scissors, liposuction cannula, loupes and hair transplant forceps. Keep them or drop them?
3. **Merges:** Mosquito, Kelly and Crile/Pean are under *Artery forceps*. Bishop-Harmon is under *Iris forceps*. Metzenbaum/Ragnell are under *Undermining scissors*. Power punch is a separate entry. Fraises, wire brushes and micromotor are under *Motorised dermabrader*. Cryoprobe, cryoroller and cryospray are under *Cryotherapy unit*. Nail nipper and nail splitter are one entry.
4. **Needle holder lock:** Khopkar puts the lock in the proximal part, but the seminar says it is near the jaws. Khopkar was followed.
5. **BP handle No. 3:** Khopkar lists blades 10, 11, 12 and 15. The seminar adds 9, and PPT1 lists 10-17 including 15B/C.
6. **Hypodermic needle:** Khopkar uses it for chemical cauterisation of sebaceous cyst/steatocystoma. The seminar says xanthelasma.
7. **Cusco's speculum insertion:** Khopkar inserts it with the blades vertical, then rotates 90 degrees anticlockwise. The seminar enters at 9 o'clock, then rotates to 6/12 o'clock. Khopkar was followed. Khopkar says the anterior and posterior walls cannot be seen. The seminar/PPT say it shields the walls in cryosurgery.
8. **Micromotor speed:** Khopkar gives 3000-30,000 rpm. The seminar text is garbled ('3,000 and 0,000 rpm'). For power punches, Khopkar gives 2000-10,000 rpm.
9. **Probable Khopkar typos to verify:** Brown dermatome graft thickness '3-8 mm'. SW monofilament foot sites '7 common peroneal, 1 posterior tibial'. In the iontophoresis principle, 'acidic agents (cations) are repelled by cathode' contradicts basic physics, but its procedure (acidic agent under the cathode) is what the dataset uses. Iris forceps described as having a 'lock'.
10. **Dressing forceps:** Khopkar says serrated. The seminar says smooth or serrated.
11. **Punch sizes:** Khopkar gives 1.5-10 mm, PPT1 gives 1-8 mm.
12. **Standard dermatosurgery knowledge added beyond the sources:** scissor tripod grip, ring-instrument grip, punch technique steps, Sims position for Sim's speculum, and the Bard-Parker blade loading method. Please review these.
13. Dermaroller/microneedling is not an instrument entry in any source; the seminar only mentions it in passing, so it was omitted.
