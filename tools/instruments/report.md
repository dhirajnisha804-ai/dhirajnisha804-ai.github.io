## Decisions applied (dermatologist, slot 2)

- Added Khopkar Appendix I office aids: common pin, glass slide, magnifying lens, measuring tape, filter paper and cotton swab stick. They use the new category "Office aids".
- Kept the 8 entries that come only from the seminar or PPTs. Kept the merges. Where sources disagree, Khopkar is followed.
- Corrected four probable typos in the source to standard values. Each corrected entry says "corrected from source" in its tips:
  - Dermatome: graft thickness is now about 0.2-0.75 mm (0.008-0.030 inch), adjustable.
  - Iontophoresis: the drug goes under the electrode of the same charge. Tap-water iontophoresis uses both trays with polarity reversed midway.
  - Iris forceps: the 'lock' is removed.
  - SW monofilament: the foot test now uses the standard 10 sites.
- Set the image field to empty for the 5 apparatus listed above.

## Remaining source differences (Khopkar followed)

1. **Needle holder lock:** Khopkar says proximal; the seminar says near the jaws.
2. **BP handle No. 3 blades:** Khopkar lists 10, 11, 12 and 15. The seminar adds 9. PPT1 lists 10-17.
3. **Hypodermic needle cauterisation:** Khopkar says sebaceous cyst or steatocystoma. The seminar says xanthelasma.
4. **Cusco's speculum insertion:** Khopkar inserts with the blades vertical, then rotates 90 degrees anticlockwise. The seminar enters at 9 o'clock, then rotates to 6/12. On wall visibility, the seminar and PPT say the blades protect the walls during cryosurgery.
5. **Micromotor speed:** Khopkar gives 3000-30,000 rpm. The seminar's figure is garbled.
6. **Dressing forceps:** Khopkar says serrated. The seminar says smooth or serrated.
7. **Punch sizes:** Khopkar gives 1.5-10 mm. PPT1 gives 1-8 mm.
8. **Additions from standard dermatosurgery knowledge (please review):** scissor and ring-instrument grips, punch biopsy steps, the Sims position, the blade loading method, the standard foot sites and the iontophoresis physics.
